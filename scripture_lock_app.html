Biblical History 📖

Creation - 1800 BC

Creation to Patriarchs

God creates the world, humanity falls into sin, and God begins His redemptive plan through the patriarchs.

Genesis 1-11
1800-1400 BC

Patriarchs and Egypt

Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph - God's covenant people grow in Egypt.

Genesis 12-50
1400-1380 BC

Exodus and Law

Moses leads Israel out of Egypt, receives the Law at Sinai, and wanders in the wilderness.

Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy
1380-1050 BC

Conquest and Judges

Joshua leads Israel into Canaan, followed by the period of the Judges.

Joshua, Judges, Ruth
1050-930 BC

United Kingdom

Saul, David, and Solomon rule over a united Israel.

1 & 2 Samuel, 1 Kings 1-11, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles 1-9
930-538 BC

Divided Kingdom and Exile

Israel divides into northern and southern kingdoms, prophets warn of coming judgment, and both kingdoms go into exile.

1 Kings 12-22, 2 Kings, 2 Chronicles 10-36, Major & Minor Prophets
538-400 BC

Return and Restoration

Exiles return to Jerusalem, rebuild the temple and walls, and prepare for the coming Messiah.

Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Malachi
6 BC - 30 AD

Life of Jesus

The birth, ministry, death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

Matthew, Mark, Luke, John
30-100 AD

Early Church

The Holy Spirit descends at Pentecost, the gospel spreads, and churches are established throughout the Roman Empire.

Acts, New Testament Epistles, Revelation

Church History ⛪

33-313 AD

The Underground Revolution

From a small Jewish sect to a movement that challenged the Roman Empire, often through martyrdom and persecution.

Acts 2:42-47, 4:32-35
313-500 AD

Truth or Consequences

Christianity becomes legal, leading to theological clarifications through ecumenical councils against heresies.

500-1500 AD

Light in the Darkness

Monasticism preserves knowledge, missionaries spread the faith, and the church wrestles with corruption and reform.

1500-1650 AD

The Great Reformation

Rediscovery of justification by faith leads to Protestant Reformation and Catholic Counter-Reformation.

1650-Present

Global Fire

Revivals, missionary movements, and global expansion of Christianity, particularly in the Global South.

Matthew 28:19-20

World History 🌍

3500-500 BC

Ancient Near East

Mesopotamia, Egypt, and Persia - the civilizations that formed the backdrop of the Old Testament.

800 BC-500 AD

Classical Civilizations

Greece and Rome - the world into which Christianity was born and spread.

300-1900 AD

African Civilizations

The often overlooked Christian heritage of Africa including Ethiopia, Nubia, and the Kingdom of Kongo.

500-1500 AD

Medieval Period

Islamic expansion, Charlemagne's empire, Crusades, and the Mongols.

1400-1800 AD

Age of Exploration

European exploration, colonization, and the beginning of global missionary movements.

1700-Present

Modern Era

Enlightenment, revolutions, world wars, and the digital age - and how Christianity has adapted and thrived.

Jesus' Genealogy 🌳

The Two Genealogies

Matthew presents Joseph's lineage (legal right to throne), while Luke presents Mary's lineage (biological descent from David).

Matthew 1:1-17; Luke 3:23-38

The Abrahamic Line

Jesus fulfills God's promise to Abraham that through his offspring all nations would be blessed.

Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16

The Davidic Covenant

Jesus is the promised eternal king from David's line who would establish God's everlasting kingdom.

2 Samuel 7:12-16; Isaiah 9:6-7

The Women in the Genealogy

Tamar, Rahab, Ruth, and Bathsheba - women with unusual stories showing God's grace to outsiders.

Matthew 1:3, 5-6

The Exile and Return

The genealogy shows God's faithfulness despite judgment and exile, preserving the line of the Messiah.

Matthew 1:11-12, 17

Prophetic Fulfillment

Every detail of Jesus' ancestry fulfills specific prophecies about the Messiah's origins.

Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1
